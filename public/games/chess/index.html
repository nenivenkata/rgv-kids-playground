How to play & piece guide

In computer mode you play White. In two-player mode, take turns sharing this computer. Tap your piece, then a highlighted square. Capture the opponent’s pieces and protect your king.

♙ Pawn: forward one, or two on its first move; captures diagonally.
♖ Rook: straight lines. ♗ Bishop: diagonals.
♕ Queen: straight lines and diagonals.
♘ Knight: an L shape; it can jump.
♔ King: one square; it must stay out of check.

Check means your king is attacked. Checkmate ends the game. Castling, en passant, and pawn promotion are supported. Undo lets you try a different plan. The computer is a simple practice opponent.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, use arrow keys between squares, then Enter or Space to select. Board flipping changes the display, not whose turn it is.
