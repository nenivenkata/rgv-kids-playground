These lessons use spoken letter names and whole-word examples. The sound symbols are a reading guide; browser voices do not reliably speak isolated phonemes. Voice availability depends on your browser and installed voices; some voices need internet. Miss Maya’s mouth animation follows speech activity, not precise lip movements. No microphone is used.
