ONE LITTLE WORD AT A TIME

Listen. Build. Discover.

Grown-up phonics guide

Say the word, model its sounds, then blend them together. The vowel marks below mean short vowel sounds. Say the sounds without adding “uh” to consonants.

The speaker reads whole words, not individual phoneme recordings. Letter names and letter sounds are different. Use the guide to model sounds together. Voices vary by browser and accent; some require internet. All activities also work with a grown-up reading aloud.

Word families group words with the same ending sound, such as cat and hat. This is a short practice activity, not a complete reading curriculum.
